# Online algorithm adoption record

The online additions preserve existing processor interfaces and defaults. This record separates implemented behavior from research candidates because a causal design, source licence, or published result alone does not establish a compatible integration. The [ecosystem catalogue](ecosystem.json) records the source snapshots; the [processing guide](online-processing.rst) explains the public processors.

**Implemented** means that code and regression tests are present. **Covered** means that existing behavior meets the specific requirement and has regression coverage. **Deferred** identifies work that remains outside this integration. A commit value of `null` identifies an issue discussion, not a source-code revision. The listed commits identify inspected external sources; they do not imply that those commits were merged.

The new bar decoder reuses madmom's existing bar-pointer state and transition models. `CausalDownBeatActivationProcessor` supplies an untrained bass-accent experiment; it does not supply a pretrained causal downbeat network. The bundled bidirectional downbeat networks still use future context. Bass accents can be syncopated, and equal kicks cannot establish bar starts. These limits prevent a claim that the external causal model integrations are complete.

The robust period and rhythm scores measure periodic agreement, not music identity. Periodic speech or noise can pass them. Meter priors and octave ranges express caller preferences; they do not prove the correct meter or metrical level. Before changing defaults, compare median and trimmed-mean interval baselines, pulse-phase continuity, tempo adaptation, absence behavior, and added processing cost on development recordings. Synthetic and compatibility tests establish implementation behavior; they do not establish superiority over the external methods.
